Siyaasadda Asturnaanta (Soomaali)

La cusboonaysiiyay: 28 Oktoobar 2026

Dukaanka waa app lagu maareeyo dukaanka: iib, keyd, qarash, deymo iyo rasiidyo. App-ka waxaa bixiya Maajid Design, Kismaayo, Soomaaliya.

1. Xogta aan ururino

  • Akoonka: email-kaaga, iyo magaca akoonka Google haddii aad Google ku gasho.
  • Xogta dukaanka ee aad gelisid: iib, alaab, keyd, qarash, deymo (magaca macmiilka iyo lambarka telefoonka haddii aad gelisid) iyo rasiidyo.
  • Xogta tijaabada iyo lacag-bixinta: taariikhda aad bilowday iyo taariikhda lagu bixiyay. Lacagta laftigeeda app-ka gudihiisa lagama qaado.

Ma ururinno goobta aad joogto, sawirada, xiriirrada telefoonkaaga, ama xog xayeysiis. Ma isticmaalno adeegyada xayeysiiska ama falanqaynta (analytics).

2. Sida aan u isticmaalno

Xogta waxaan u isticmaalnaa oo keliya si app-ku u shaqeeyo: gelitaanka, keydinta xogtaada, isku-waafajinta taleefannada, iyo xaqiijinta tijaabada ama lacag-bixinta. Xogtaada ma iibinno, mana wadaajinno.

3. Meesha lagu keydiyo

Xogta waxaa lagu keydiyaa Google Firebase (Authentication iyo Cloud Firestore), iyo taleefankaaga gudihiisa (si app-ku internet la'aan ugu shaqeeyo). Google waa adeeg-bixiyaha keydinta.

4. Kala gaarsanaanta xogta

Dukaan kasta xogtiisa keliya ayuu arkaa. Xeerarka amniga ee database-ka ayaa ogolaanaya in akoon kasta uu gaadho xogtiisa keliya. Si daacad ah: horumariyaha app-ka, isagoo leh gelitaan farsamo oo Firebase ah, waxaa laga yaabaa inuu arko xogta keydsan. Waxaan u isticmaalnaa kaliya taageero, dayactir iyo codsi aad adigu bixiso.

5. Wadaagid

Rasiid markaad ku dirto WhatsApp, adigu ayaa dira. Xogta kale ma siinno qof saddexaad, marka laga reebo Google (adeeg-bixiyaha keydinta) ama marka sharci u baahdo.

6. Amniga

Isgaarsiintu waxay maraysaa HTTPS, gelitaanku wuxuu u baahan yahay akoon, xeerarka database-kuna waxay xannibaan gelitaanka qof kale. Ma jirto nidaam gebi ahaanba ammaan ah, laakiin waxaan isku daynaa inaan ilaalinno xogtaada.

7. Hayn iyo tirtirid

Xogta waa la hayaa inta akoonkaagu furan yahay. Si aad u tirtirto akoonkaaga iyo xogtaada oo dhan, u dir email faysaladan091@gmail.com adigoo isticmaalaya email-ka akoonka, oo ku qor "Tirtir akoonkayga". Waan tirtiraynaa 30 maalmood gudahood. Waxaad sidoo kale soo dejin kartaa Backup (Aniga → Soo deji Backup) kahor tirtirka.

8. Carruurta

App-ku looma talagalin carruurta ka yar 13 jir, mana ururino xog ay ku saabsan yihiin si ula kac ah.

9. Isbeddel

Siyaasaddan waan cusboonaysiin karnaa. Isbeddelka waxaa lagu qoraa bogga halkan, taariikhda kore ayaana la beddelayaa.

10. Xiriir

Su'aalo: faysaladan091@gmail.com

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Dukaanka is a shop-management app for sales, stock, expenses, debts and receipts. The app is provided by Maajid Design, Kismayo, Somalia.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your Google account name if you sign in with Google.
  • Shop data you enter: sales, products, stock, expenses, debts (customer names and phone numbers if you enter them) and receipts.
  • Trial and payment status: the date you started and the date until which you have paid. Payments themselves are not processed inside the app.

We do not collect your location, photos, phone contacts, or advertising data. We do not use advertising or analytics services.

2. How we use it

We use your data only to run the app: sign-in, saving your data, syncing between your devices, and verifying your trial or payment. We do not sell or share your data.

3. Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and on your own device so the app works offline. Google acts as our storage provider.

4. Separation of data

Each merchant sees only their own data. Database security rules allow each account to access only its own records. To be transparent: the app developer, who has technical access to the Firebase console, may be able to view stored data. We use this access only for support, maintenance and requests you make.

5. Sharing

When you send a receipt via WhatsApp, you send it yourself. We do not share other data with third parties, except Google as our storage provider, or when required by law.

6. Security

Data travels over HTTPS, sign-in is required, and database rules block access by others. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and all your data, email faysaladan091@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days. You can download a backup first (Aniga → Soo deji Backup).

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page and the date above will be updated.
